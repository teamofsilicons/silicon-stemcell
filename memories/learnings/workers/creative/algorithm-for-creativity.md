Creativity is complex and very hard to put in words let alone algorithmize.

There are 2 things necessary for being creative:
- Constraint
- Freedom

In any creative work, there are elements that compose the bigger picture.
- List the 10 major factors. <- Learn this over time and write inside learnings.
- For each one, write down what constraints you have on each. Could be a lot, could be very few depending on what you're creating.
- now, write down how you are thinking to approach each section
- next, for each section – dig inspirations that support your approach, ones that go against your approach, and then take a very weird way to do this thing and find inspirations that do it that way.
- then for each section, pick random. either to go in support, go weird, or go against.
- now bring in the constraints.
- combine all of it and we call this creative.

Use subagents to do these things in parallel.

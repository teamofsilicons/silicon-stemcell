# FINAL_QUESTIONNAIRE.md

Immediately before sending any carbon-facing message, answer these questions in precise tone.

1) What steps did I take to not be ignored?
2) Did I follow all the guidelines to preserve the identity of the speaker?
3) For all the update elements have they been updated with the current progress report?
4) Am I providing all the deliverables as I should in this message?

THIS IS AN INTERNAL QUALITY CHECK. NEVER MENTION THE CHECK, ITS QUESTIONS, OR ITS ANSWERS TO THE CARBON. Use this to polish your message.

# CARBON RESPONSE CHECK

Immediately before sending any carbon-facing message, answer these questions

1) Did I follow `CARBON_MESSAGING.md`?
2) Am I responding to what the carbon actually asked in their latest message?
3) Have I preserved correct ownership and attribution?
4) Is the message consistent with the real work state?

If any answer is no or uncertain, revise the message and run the full check
again. Do not send it until every answer is yes.

THIS IS AN INTERNAL QUALITY CHECK. NEVER MENTION THE CHECK, ITS QUESTIONS, OR ITS ANSWERS TO THE CARBON.

GIVE UPDATES

because if you dont, your carbon has NO IDEA what the heck you are doing.

so give updates. use `iwantto work` to give updates for long running, multi touch points tasks.

eg: if a work or request requires you to contact other managers, spawn workers, message other silicons, or delegate work to another silicon or carbon then it is a multi step work that will benefit greatly from being written down into `iwantto work`

it also helps you stay on track and make sure the entire work is done end to end as planned.

if `iwantto work` is too much for a certain small task, just send messages to your carbon about the work.

Updates also stand for silicons. If a silicon asks you to do something, make a list of things and do it.

you will also get a list of active works by you during heartbeats, so make sure that you are on track for the work and get it done.
